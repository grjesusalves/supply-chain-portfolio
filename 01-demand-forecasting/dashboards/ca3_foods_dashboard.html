What to do with this

  • Use LightGBM for CA_3 food replenishment planning.
  • Keep the lag-28 forecast as the benchmark.
  • Do not claim dollar savings. None were calculated.
  • FOODS_3_090 is the exception: baseline WAPE 0.1975 beats LightGBM 0.2130 (training units 250,502; holdout units 3,357).
  • Out of scope: other stores, other categories, inventory policy (project 02).

A high forecast is extra inventory, not protection against stockouts. LightGBM is more high than the baseline (+2.6% vs +1.1%) even though its absolute error is smaller. About 66% of California item-days are zeros, which is why WAPE is the headline and MAPE is not.
