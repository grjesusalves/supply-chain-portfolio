Late delivery is the shipping mode

Question: which lines miss the promised date, and does a classifier beat a rule that only knows the shipping mode? Answer: manage the mode. On 172,765 non-canceled lines, 98,977 are late. First Class is late on every one of its 26,513 lines. The logistic model and the random forest did not beat that mode rule.

Region, once mode is fixed

Region does not move the late rate once shipping mode is fixed: First Class is late on every non-canceled line in all 23 regions and all 5 markets, and median slip is 1 day in every region. The largest gap between a region’s late rate and the rate implied by its own mode mix is Canada, −0.06522236464088049, on 907 lines.

What to do

  • Fix the First Class promise or the operation. The promise is 1 day. Delivery is 2 days on all 26,513 non-canceled First Class lines.
  • Treat Second Class as the other mode problem: 26,987 / 33,806 late, median slip 2.
  • Do not deploy the logistic regression or the random forest. They lost to the shipping-mode baseline.
  • Do not set an operating threshold on either model.
  • Do not quote a dollar penalty. The file has no late-delivery cost. Dollar impact is not estimated.
