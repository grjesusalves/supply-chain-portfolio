Median freight per kg by named mode

Charter looks cheap on the median because of size. Median weight is 3,289 kg on air charter and 656 kg on air. The regression is not shown as a live model on this page. Holding weight, that log model puts charter about 66% above air. After rates above $67.65 per kg are dropped (the weighed-set 95th percentile), the cautious figure is about 21%, not 66%. It is still not a saving.

Median versus mean: the mean is the tail

The mean sits next to the median so $38.93 is not read as a typical shipment. Mean ÷ median is how far the tail pulls that row. The maximum is $31,088 per kg on DN-1683, air charter, 4 kg, freight $124,351 (exact rate 31087.705, exact freight 124350.82).

Dollars are historical, 2006–2015. This is an upper bound, not a booked saving.

What not to conclude

  • Do not call a country expensive off an uncontrolled median. Among countries with at least 20 weighed shipments, Botswana leads that list (median $38.94 on 62). Inside air and the 500–5,000 kg band that count is 7. Seven is under the rank threshold of 20.
  • Do not plan on the 66 percent charter gap. The cautious association, after dropping rates above $67.65 per kg, is about 21 percent, and it is still not a saving.
  • Do not switch a lane to ocean because the ocean median is $1.68. Ocean's cheap median is tangled with DDP.
  • Do not headline the mean of $38.93. The median, $7.26, is the typical weighed shipment.
