Weekly operating review — logistics

Historical extract of Olist Brazilian e-commerce. Not a live feed. Delivered purchases run from 15 Sep 2016 through 29 Aug 2018. All purchases in the file run from 4 Sep 2016 through 17 Oct 2018. Currency is Brazilian reais. Nothing on this page is converted.

1. Orders purchased that week

99,441 orders were purchased, across 101 weeks starting Monday 2016-08-29 through Monday 2018-10-15. This count is every order, not only the 96,470 that were delivered.

The first point is the Monday of the week that contains 4 Sep 2016, which is a Sunday, so that Monday is 29 Aug 2016. The last week is partial too. 10 of these weeks have purchases and no delivered order. They stay on this chart, and the time charts below leave them blank. 11 Mondays between the first and last purchase week have no purchase. Those are gaps in the line, not zeros.

2. Median purchase-to-door days, and median transit days

On 96,470 delivered orders, median purchase-to-door time is 10.2 days and median transit, carrier to door, is 7.1 days. Each point is that week’s median, not a mean, and not an average of other weeks.

Transit omits negative carrier-to-door durations (23 orders in the extract). Those orders stay in the purchase-to-door median and in the delivered order count. A week with no delivered order is left blank. It is not drawn as zero days.

3. On-time rate against the estimated delivery date

89,936 of 96,470 delivered orders (93.2%) arrived on or before the estimate’s calendar date. The estimate is stored at midnight, so the comparison is the calendar date, not the timestamp.

The rate is on-time orders divided by delivered orders that week. It is not a substitute for the days in the previous section.

4. Total freight and median freight per item

Delivered items, 110,189 of them, carry R$2,202,835.80 of freight. The median item is R$16.26. Items with no distance stay in this total. They are not in the band table.

Freight is the item, in reais. Undelivered orders are not given a freight line on this page.

5. Share of items that cross a state line

70,328 of 110,189 delivered items (63.8%) have a customer state different from the seller state.

6. Distance bands

95,992 delivered orders have a distance. The order’s band is its farthest seller. Median freight is the item’s own distance, on 109,651 items. 478 delivered orders have no distance. They are not in this table.

7. Customers by state, next to sellers by state

96,096 customer people and 3,095 sellers. São Paulo is 41.9% of the people (40,302 / 96,096) and 59.7% of the sellers (1,849 / 3,095).

People are counted once per state they appear in, so the state rows sum to more than 96,096 distinct people. The percents use the distinct-people denominator and the full seller table. Alagoas, Amapá, Roraima, and Tocantins have customers and no sellers. This is coverage, not a warehouse site.
