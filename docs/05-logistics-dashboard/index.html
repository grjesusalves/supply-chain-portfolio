1 · Weekly trend

Service is not steady week to week: in the week of 26 Feb 2018 the median wait reached 15.8 days and only 73.7% arrived on time, against 10.2 days and 93.2% for the whole extract

Volume built through 2017 and peaked at 2,915 delivered orders in the week of 20 Nov 2017. The extract-level figures are computed on orders, not averaged across weeks, so a good total can hide weak weeks.

What leadership should watch

Three separate conversations. None of them is a savings estimate.

  1. Coverage is uneven, and 63.8% of items cross a state

    São Paulo has 59.7% of sellers and 41.9% of customers; four states have customers and no sellers. Track where coverage is thin. This is a coverage view, not a case for a new building.

  2. The extra days are in transit, not handling

    Median transit runs from 2.0 days under 50 km to 13.2 days at 1,000+ km, while seller handling stays at 1.7–1.9 days. When a destination is slow, take it to the transit conversation, not a seller-handling program.

  3. The promise stretches, so on-time can look fine while the wait is long

    On-time is 93.2% overall and 89.6% in the 1,000+ km band, where the median wait is 16.4 days. Report median days next to the on-time rate, never the rate alone.

Definitions

Delivered order
Order with status delivered and a door (customer delivery) timestamp after purchase: 96,470 orders, 110,189 items.
Purchase-to-door
Purchase timestamp to door timestamp, in days. Headline is the median.
Handling
Payment approval to carrier handoff. Negative durations excluded from the median.
Transit
Carrier handoff to door. Negative durations (23 orders) excluded from the median.
On time
Delivered on or before the estimated delivery date, compared by calendar date (89,936 / 96,470).
Cross-state item
Delivered item whose seller state differs from the customer state.
Distance
Straight-line km between zip-prefix centroids of seller and customer. Not road distance.
Distance band
0–50, 50–200, 200–500, 500–1,000, 1,000+ km, right-open, frozen. Orders use the farthest seller; freight uses the item’s own distance.
Freight
Freight value on delivered items, BRL. Total R$2,202,835.80; median per item R$16.26.
Week
Purchase week starting Monday. Weeks with no purchase are gaps, not zeros.

Data source

Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset (Olist; also published on Kaggle as olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce). Used for non-commercial purposes with attribution. Historical extract: delivered purchases 15 Sep 2016 to 29 Aug 2018; all purchases in the file run 4 Sep 2016 to 17 Oct 2018.

Method notes

  • Medians use the two-central-value rule and are computed on orders or items, never averaged across weeks.
  • Customer shares use the distinct-people denominator (96,096); state rows sum to 96,136 because 39 people appear in two states.
  • Week, band and state are different grains and are shown in separate exhibits; they are not joined.
  • Review scores are out of scope for this page.
  • Filters, ranges, sorting and highlights only change the view. They do not change any definition, population or full-period figure; a selected range shows sums and ratios of weekly counts, never a median of weekly medians.
